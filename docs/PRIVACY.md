# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 13, 2026**

## 1. Overview

ProxyStar is an Android application for discovering public proxy servers and evaluating their connectivity and latency.

This document explains important privacy considerations when using the application and public proxy services.

## 2. Information and Network Activity

ProxyStar interacts with proxy endpoints and network services as part of its functionality.

Depending on the application's implementation and the features being used, network requests may expose technical information such as an IP address, connection metadata, or request details to the relevant service.

The actual data processed by the application depends on its implementation and enabled features.

## 3. Third-Party Proxy Servers

ProxyStar may provide access to public proxy endpoints operated by third parties.

These endpoints are not necessarily owned or controlled by the ProxyStar developer. Their operators may have different logging, retention, security, and privacy practices.

A proxy operator may be able to observe connection metadata or traffic that is not protected by end-to-end encryption. HTTPS protects data in transit between your device and the destination when properly configured, but it does not make an untrusted proxy universally safe.

Do not use an unknown proxy for sensitive activities unless you understand and accept the associated risks.

## 4. Permissions and Device Data

Review the permissions requested by the installed application and grant only those you understand and consider necessary.

This policy does not claim that the application or its third-party libraries never process device information. Such claims require verification against the actual released APK and its behavior.

## 5. Data Sharing and Retention

Data handling can depend on the application's implementation and the services it contacts.

This policy does not make an unverified claim that no information is collected, transmitted, stored, or shared. Users should consider the privacy practices of any third-party proxy service they choose to use.

## 6. Security

Download ProxyStar only from the official release page:

https://github.com/DevQissy/ProxyStar/releases

Where available, verify the APK checksum against a value published through a trusted channel. A matching checksum confirms file integrity relative to that published value; it does not prove that the application is harmless.

## 7. Children's Privacy

ProxyStar is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users and guardians should evaluate whether the application and any proxy services used are appropriate for their circumstances.

This document may be updated when the application's functionality, data handling, or distribution changes. The revision date will be updated when material changes are made.
